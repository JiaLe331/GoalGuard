GoalGuard identity study · 03 September 2026

Protection, without hiding the purpose.

Four abstract combination systems and an expanded mascot study built from open boundaries, visible cores, and deliberate forward space. The second mascot round explicitly moves beyond the lime side-profile animal formula.

One visual rule

The user’s goal stays visible. Protection shapes organise around it, never turn it into a lock, shield, token, or guarantee badge.

SELECTED MASCOT · COLOUR STUDY

Pip keeps the lime

Five harmonious treatments tested whether electric lime should fill Pip or act only as the purpose signal. Electric Lime is now selected for production, with posture and expression providing the distinctive character.

Electric Lime selected, alternates archived

Electric Lime is the production colourway. Soft Lime and Cloud Neutral remain design-system-compatible studies; Meadow Mint and Quiet Periwinkle remain illustration and campaign explorations.
