# GoalGuard logo exploration

This directory is an isolated brand study. Nothing here is imported by the production Next.js application.

**Current selection:** Pip the Pangolin in **Electric Lime**: electric-lime body, near-black armour, white facial contrast, and an electric-lime purpose point inside the white belly field.

## Core specification

- Palette: `#0B0C0A`, `#C9F52B`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F2F3F0`, from `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.
- Wordmark: Manrope 700 with system fallbacks. The selected direction should be converted to final outlined letterforms before trademark filing or vendor handoff.
- Symbol grid: 64×64 with a nominal 6-unit safe area.
- Clear space: at least one lime-core diameter around the mark or lockup.
- Minimum recommended size: 16px for the symbol and 120px for the horizontal lockup.
- Backgrounds: colour marks are intended for white/light surfaces; reversed marks use white plus lime on near-black; lime surfaces use the black-only mark.

## Quality notes

- Every mark uses filled geometry or an 8–10 unit stroke, so its silhouette survives at 16px.
- Colour is not required for recognition: the `variants.svg` boards include black-only and white-only tests.
- No option uses a coin, token, padlock, literal shield, checkmark, or promise-like security badge.
- The open forms intentionally communicate guidance and bounded protection rather than guaranteed outcomes.
- SVG backgrounds are transparent except `favicon.svg` and the labelled comparison surfaces.

## Review recommendation

Proceed with **Pip the Pangolin** in **Electric Lime** as the production mascot system. Use near-black armour on light surfaces, reversed white armour on dark surfaces, and the black-only mark on lime. **Purpose Orbit** remains the quietest abstract fallback, while the other mascot and colour directions remain archived exploration. Use a 2D master mascot; reserve any future 3D treatment for campaign imagery.
