GoalGuard · Mascot exploration round two

Five different reasons for a mascot to exist.

This round moves away from the lime side-profile animal formula. Every character keeps the user’s purpose visible, but each uses a genuinely different silhouette and emotional role.

Design correction

Pip’s species is original, but the lime head, round eye, smile, and side profile are too close to CoinGecko’s category cues. The alternatives below test protection, care, clarity, and calm movement without resembling a crypto lizard.

01 · BASELINE

Pip

The patient armour

Layered scales stay open around the purpose point. Pip listens and explains; it never celebrates, predicts, or approves a trade.

Warmest companionBut the closest to CoinGecko’s visual category. Do not advance unchanged.

02 · STEADINESS

Tavi

The deliberate guardian

The shell panels make bounded protection tangible, while the front view feels stable and avoids the crypto side-profile convention.

Clearest protection cueWatch for a generic insurance feel and the unintended idea of slowness.

03 · CANOPY

Miro

The calm navigator

Broad wings form a protective canopy around the purpose point. The manta moves through complexity without looking aggressive.

Strongest recommendationMost distinctive silhouette, least crypto-cliché, and naturally motion-ready.

04 · CARE

Nook

The purpose builder

The bird builds an open nest around the goal. It makes protection feel like preparation and care rather than an absolute guarantee.

Best human storyExcellent for goal creation, but needs a simpler micro-mark for small UI.

05 · CLARITY

Lumi

The visible guide

The purpose point becomes a beacon while the wings act as open brackets. Lumi helps users see what matters without implying control.

Best secondary helperCompact and UI-friendly, though the protection metaphor is less direct.

Recommended system

2D master mascot

Crisp at 24px, reliable in light/dark/lime, easy to animate accessibly, and aligned with GoalGuard’s flat code-native visual language.

Optional derivative

3D for campaigns only

Useful later for a pitch-deck cover, launch still, or booth visual. Keep it out of navigation, financial states, and the core workflow.

Exploration only · vector geometry remains editable · the lime point always represents the user’s purpose
